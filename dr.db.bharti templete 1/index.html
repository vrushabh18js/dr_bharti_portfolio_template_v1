About Prof. (Dr.) Devidas B. Bharati

Director & Professor • Savitribai Phule Pune University Affiliated

Prof. (Dr.) Devidas B. Bharati is an accomplished academic leader, former Director, and recognized Ph.D. guide with over 43 years of dedicated service in higher education under Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU). An authority in Banking & Finance, Advanced Accountancy, Costing, and Direct/Indirect Taxation, he has shaped generations of management professionals and researchers.

Throughout his 43-year career, Dr. Bharati served for 24 years at the prestigious Brihan Maharashtra College of Commerce (BMCC), Pune, followed by leadership as Director at Rajgad Institute of Management Research & Development and key roles at Allana Institute of Management Sciences and Pratibha Institute of Business Management. A former Academic Council Member and Chairman of the Finance Board at SPPU, he has guided 12 Ph.D. and 16 M.Phil. scholars to completion, authored 16 books, and published over 100 research papers.
